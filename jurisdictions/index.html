Jurisdictions

Public Relations & Communications jurisdictional reference records

This directory provides access to jurisdictional reference records for Public Relations & Communications. Each record describes the commercial service-line in its relevant country, region, state or province, including market structure, public affairs, consumer communications, advertising and influencer disclosure, data and direct marketing, operating constraints and cross-border considerations.

Jurisdictional records are editorial references. They are designed to support initial market mapping and agency, corporate-affairs or communications planning. They do not replace legal, regulatory, privacy, consumer, lobbying, financial, healthcare, advertising or professional advice for a specific engagement.
